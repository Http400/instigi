# Instigi - workouts tracker

This application helps users track and manage their workouts in a simple, structured way. It allows users to log exercises, monitor progress over time, and stay consistent with their fitness goals through personalized workout history and performance insights. The app is designed to make workout tracking fast, intuitive, and motivating for both beginners and experienced athletes.

## Problem statement

Tracking workouts manually is time-consuming, disorganized, and difficult to maintain over time. Existing fitness apps are often overly complex, filled with unnecessary features, or not flexible enough for personalized workout tracking.

This application solves that problem by providing a simple and efficient way to record workouts, track exercise performance, and monitor progress in one place. It helps users stay motivated, organized, and accountable by making fitness tracking quick, accessible, and easy to understand.

The primary focus is delivering a seamless tracking experience that encourages consistency and helps users stay motivated in achieving their fitness goals

## The scope of MVP

- User Authentication
  - Sign up, login, and secure account management.
- Exercise Management
  - Predefined exercise list with the ability to add custom exercises.
- Workout Logging
  - Create and record workout sessions.
- Workout History
  - View previously completed workouts and exercise records.
- Progress Tracking
  - Track improvements in weight, reps, workout frequency, and consistency over time.
- Simple Dashboard
  - Quick overview of recent workouts and basic statistics.
- Mobile-Friendly User Experience
  - Fast, intuitive, and distraction-free interface optimized for mobile use.

## What is not included in the scope of MVP

- Workouts sharing
- Friend system
- Competitive challenges
- Coaching system
- Mobile app (only web app at the beginning)

## Success criteria

- Task Completion Speed – How quickly and easily users can log a workout session.
