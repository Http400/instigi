# Vision

## Problem Statement
Many people struggle to stay consistent with their fitness routines because tracking workouts manually is time-consuming, disorganized, and difficult to maintain. Existing fitness apps are often overly complex, filled with unnecessary features, or too rigid for personalized tracking. This app solves that by providing a simple, efficient way to record workouts, track performance, and monitor progress in one place.

## Target Users
Fitness enthusiasts, gym-goers, and individuals who want to maintain a consistent workout routine — from beginners who need a simple starting point to experienced athletes tracking strength gains and workout history. Users value convenience, motivation, and measurable progress. They primarily use mobile devices during or after workouts and prefer a fast, intuitive, distraction-free experience.

## Core Value Proposition
Making workout tracking **fast, simple, and reliable**. Users can quickly log exercises, sets, reps, weights, and sessions with minimal effort, and get a clear view of their progress over time. The primary goal is a seamless tracking experience that encourages consistency.

## Success Metrics
- **User Retention Rate** (primary KPI)
- **Daily Active Users (DAU)**
- **Monthly Active Users (MAU)**

## MVP Scope

### Must Have (MVP)
- User Authentication (sign up, login, secure account management)
- Workout Logging (create sessions; add exercises, sets, reps, duration, weights)
- Exercise Management (predefined exercise library + ability to add custom exercises)
- Workout History (view previously completed sessions and exercise records)
- Progress Tracking — simple totals and counts only (total workouts, last weight used per exercise, workout frequency)
- Simple Dashboard (quick overview of recent workouts and basic stats)
- Mobile-friendly UI (fast, intuitive, distraction-free, optimized for mobile)

### Nice to Have (Future)
- Workout Plans & Templates (reusable routines and training programs)
- Advanced Analytics (charts, trends, personal records, performance insights)
- Social Features (sharing, friends, community challenges)
- Gamification (streaks, achievements, badges)
- Wearable & Health App Integration (Apple Health, Google Fit, smartwatches)
- AI-Based Recommendations (personalized and adaptive training plans)
- Nutrition & Calorie Tracking
- Offline Mode
- Coach / Trainer Features
- Notifications & Reminders
